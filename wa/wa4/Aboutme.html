I'm currently a student at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I'm finishing my degree in Media Production with minors in German Studies and Creative Technology and Design. Alongside my studies, I also work an Audio Visual Technician at
the Glenn Miller Ballroom, where I support live events using sound and lighting systems.

Outside of my classes and work, I like to keep my creative side engaged, through creating absract digital art, broadcasting music on Radio 1190, colaborating in creative efforts with my friends, and attending DIY Music events locally in Boulder and Denver.
